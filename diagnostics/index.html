Data and method

HoldingsEach fund house's statutory monthly portfolio (SEBI format), read stock by stock and matched by ISIN.
OverlapFor two funds: the sum, over stocks both hold, of the smaller of the two weights (% of net assets). 0% means no common stocks; 100% means identical portfolios. The headline shows your closest pair; the portfolio figure is the average across all pairs, weighted by the money in each fund.
Stocks countedIndian and overseas equity shares. Hedged arbitrage positions, REITs/InvITs, bonds, money-market instruments and cash are left out.
Company sizeAMFI's half-yearly list: top 100 by market value = large cap, 101–250 = mid cap, 251+ = small cap.
Expense ratioTotal Expense Ratio for each scheme, Regular and Direct plan, as filed daily with AMFI: base expense ratio + brokerage + GST and other statutory levies. The base ratio is shown in brackets.
ProjectionYour current value plus monthly SIP, compounded at your assumed return minus each plan's expense ratio. An illustration, not a forecast.
